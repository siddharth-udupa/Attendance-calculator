Help Guide

Simple 3-Step Setup

Quickly calculate and predict your attendance status.

1 Step 1: Add Your Subjects

Open Subjects from the menu. Type each class name and press + to add it.

2 Step 2: Build Your Timetable

Open Timetable and tap period slots to assign subjects for each day of the week.

3 Step 3: Mark Daily Attendance

Open Attendance and tap a subject card to mark it Present, Absent, cancelled, or clear.

Detailed Feature Guide

Dashboard

Displays your total attendance percentage, subject cards, and automatic predictors that show how many classes you need to maintain your target.

Subjects

Add or delete subjects. Changes update dynamically across your timetable and calendar.

Timetable

Build your weekly schedule. Click + to add period slots for any day of the week.

Attendance & Markers

Tap calendar dates to view or edit logs. Set a Start Marker to define when logging begins, and use bulk actions for quick marking.
